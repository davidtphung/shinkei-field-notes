THE SPECIFIC PROMISE

Not more seafood.
Better seafood.

Fishing has never lacked effort. It has lacked a system designed to protect what matters after the catch. Shinkei brings robotics and computer vision to an artisanal Japanese harvest practice, so fishermen can retain value, buyers can see provenance, and diners can taste the difference.
